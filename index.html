Your data

Readings are saved only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded and there are no accounts. Use the same browser each time, and export a backup now and then.

How the baseline works

  1. Measure the same way each time. Sit with your back supported, feet flat, arm at heart level, after 5 quiet minutes. Skip caffeine, exercise and smoking for 30 minutes.
  2. Take 2 or 3 readings a minute apart. Baseline averages them into one session.
  3. Measure morning and evening for about a week. Home-monitoring guidance suggests this and drops the first day.
  4. Tag what is going on. Only seated readings with no coffee, exercise, meal, stress, alcohol, nicotine, full bladder or illness count toward your baseline. Everything else is compared against it.
Adult blood pressure categories (mmHg)
CategorySystolicDiastolic

Categories follow the AHA/ACC guideline, unchanged in the 2025 update. A diagnosis needs averaged readings on more than one occasion. Source: 2025 AHA/ACC High Blood Pressure Guideline. “Low” (under 90/60) is an informal flag, not a guideline category.

Not medical advice. Baseline is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything. Home readings vary with the cuff, the day and your technique. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist about your numbers, and use a validated upper-arm monitor. If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, weakness, vision changes or trouble speaking, call emergency services.

Claude AI

Generate written reports and chat about your readings. This is optional and uses your own Anthropic API key, so you pay Anthropic directly for usage.

What leaves your device

When you generate a report or send a chat message, Baseline sends a summary of your readings straight from this browser to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com): dates, numbers, arm, position and the context tags you picked. It never sends your name or email, and notes are left out unless you turn them on below. Baseline has no server, so nobody else sees it.
