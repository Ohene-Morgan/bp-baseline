How your baseline is calculated

  1. Measure the same way each time. Sit with your back supported, feet flat, arm at heart level, after 5 minutes of quiet. Skip caffeine, exercise and smoking for 30 minutes.
  2. Take 2–3 readings a minute apart in the log form. They are averaged into one session.
  3. Measure morning and evening for about a week. Home monitoring guidance suggests this, and drops the first day.
  4. Tag what is going on. Only seated readings with no coffee, exercise, meal, stress, alcohol, nicotine, full bladder or illness count toward your baseline. Everything else is compared against it.

Your data stays with you

Readings are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there are no accounts. Because of that, export a backup now and then, and use the same browser each time.

Not medical advice. This tool is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything. Home readings vary with the cuff, the day and your technique. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist about your numbers, and use a validated upper-arm monitor. If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, weakness, vision changes or trouble speaking, call emergency services.
